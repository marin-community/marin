Exact first failure
===================

Range: contract_map.steady.0.ordinary_xla
Exception: RuntimeError: NVTX rejected range 'contract_map.steady.0.ordinary_xla'

The exact source declares `nvtxRangePushA` with signed `ctypes.c_int` and
rejects only results below zero. It therefore already accepts the documented
outermost level `0` and nested positive levels. Reaching the exception proves
that the pinned interop library returned a negative value, but the diagnostic
does not record which negative value.
