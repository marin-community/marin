Try to demonstrate defects in the task or its grader. Use shell and read_file to inspect and prepare candidates, and grade to test them. Finish with report. Each finding must contain a claim explaining why the grader scores a candidate wrongly, that concrete candidate (reply and files), and the expected status/reward bounds that reproduce the WRONG grader behavior. For example, an incorrect answer wrongly accepted should expect graded status with full reward; a correct answer wrongly rejected should expect the observed rejection or low reward. The validator replays each candidate against the original task and confirms a finding only if that grade meets its expected status/reward bounds.

{sandbox_limits}

Do not report suspicions, general commentary, or successful checks as findings. Return an empty findings list when no defect was demonstrated, including when the grader behaved correctly. Candidate files require the task to have a solver machine; your own inspection shell does not give the task one. Candidate paths must be normalized and relative to the task working directory. Do not modify task.json to change what is graded. A finish without report is an incomplete review.
