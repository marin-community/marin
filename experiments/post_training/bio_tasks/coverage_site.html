Inspect the first assignments

Scale the inputs, preserve the assessment

A recipe defines a scientific question, admissible real inputs, artifact contract, oracle and verifier. New tasks may reuse a recipe when they add meaningful independent data or decisions. A changed random seed or relabeled organism is not new biological diversity. Record recipe version, study lineage and transformations; reject duplicates.

There is no task-count cap, fixed instances-per-recipe quota or easy/medium/hard quota. Current generators do not yet automate this balanced allocation or unlimited observed-data sourcing.

What coverage means

Unit or stateMeaning
CompetencyAn assessable scientific ability with a stated outcome, scope and observable artifacts. Areas and families organize competencies and receive no direct task assignments.
PlannedA draft definition and question/source evidence exist. Feasibility and boundaries may need refinement.
CandidateAn existing real-data recipe exercises related operations. It can cover only part of the competency; no validation credit is implied.
ValidatedA reviewed task meets the bounded competency outcome, with exact artifact identities, a passing Harbor oracle, discriminating negative controls, and scientific/provenance review.
Release-readyThe complete publishing contract is satisfied. Validation status is attached to specific task, environment and verifier hashes.

Report both leaf breadth (competencies with a validated witness / draft competencies) and depth (distinct validated tasks and independent biological lineages per competency). Aggregate branch breadth from unique leaf IDs. A single task does not prove mastery of an entire area. Draft taxonomy changes are versioned; coverage reports retain their taxonomy version.

Task contract

  • Harbor instruction, native inputs, pinned environment, private input-reading oracle and deterministic artifact verifier. No LLM judge.
  • Declare CPU, RAM, storage, timeout, network policy and required tools. Offline by default; target solves within 30 minutes. Build-time package retrieval is separate from solve-time egress.
  • Check a correct oracle, an empty submission and plausible scientifically wrong outputs. Distinguish solution, verifier and infrastructure failure.
  • Publish input accessions, hashes, provenance, redistribution terms, transformations and biological context. Keep benchmark fixtures/answers out of authored inputs.
  • Use reusable environment/verifier identities and packed Harbor tasks for downstream ingestion, following the shared environment contract. One train split; downstream training and trace collection are separate projects.

How this draft was derived

The existing scientific workstreams supply 51 provisional outcomes and their exact source-group references. Generic data preparation is retained as cross-cutting research rather than a standalone biology quota. Three additional competencies isolate read quality, protein-function analysis and structure geometry already present in the recipe catalog. Those three currently have repository/recipe provenance only; benchmark alignment is an explicit gap. This hierarchy remains separate from the flat question-level competency review. It remains provisional; broad or compound leaves can be split after assessment review.

Operation tags are cross-cutting and currently provisional. Source-group membership identifies research provenance; it is not evidence that every source task assesses the whole competency. The explorer shows inspection depth and sample source IDs and links the full inventories.

Scientific categorization

The hierarchy is a navigation and allocation view of overlapping scientific questions. Genetics emphasizes inheritance and genetic effects; genomics emphasizes genome reconstruction and characterization. Chromatin and epigenomics is narrower than gene regulation. Phylogenetics and ecological analysis are separate. Single-cell resolution, spatial measurements, gene regulation, evolution and multiomic integration are independent facets that can apply across branches.

Facet labels describe a competency's intended scope, not demonstrated coverage by every related candidate. Selecting both single-cell and spatial requires both definition tags; it does not infer that the current task examples include a joint single-cell spatial assay.

Relationship to existing work

The task curriculum provides the reused group/capability schema. “Competency” is the display term for an assessable capability. This draft is not yet assigned upstream catalog IDs. The task environment contract defines the downstream packaging boundary. The TaskTrove coverage audit inspired this view; its source-size crediting and 1,000-task heuristic are not used here.
