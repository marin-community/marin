# Gene-length versus immune-cell expression: source assessment

Target endpoints: BixBench-Verified `bix-22-q1` (the immune-cell group with the weakest absolute gene-length/expression Pearson correlation) and `bix-22-q4` (the monocyte/CD14-group correlation). These share one original `bix-22` lineage and would receive at most one family-level coverage claim after full validation. The original CLUSTER sorted-cell matrix at [Zenodo 10000430](https://zenodo.org/records/10000430) is excluded as benchmark source material; its record also displays no license. No benchmark answers or fixtures are input candidates.

[Parse Biosciences, *Performance of Evercode WT Mini v3 in Human PBMCs*](https://www.parsebiosciences.com/datasets/performance-of-evercode-wt-mini-v3-in-human-pbmcs/) is an owner-published, observed human PBMC experiment from four healthy donors. The [author page](https://www.parsebiosciences.com/datasets/performance-of-evercode-wt-mini-v3-in-human-pbmcs/) states that Parse processed and classified cells with Azimuth plus manual annotation. The downloadable metadata CSV **does not contain cell-type labels**, and the 16,317,662 B HTML report did not expose those labels in a text scan. The source therefore supports a reproducible task only if a declared cell-classification step is added; author-assigned labels cannot be assumed to be available in the matrix. The biological unit is single cells from four donors, whereas the excluded BixBench source used sorted-cell bulk RNA sequencing. That is an explicit workflow adaptation, not the same measurement design.

## Proposed connected task, subject to source gate

Use marker-based classification of observed cells into CD4 T, CD8 T, CD14-classical monocyte, and CD19 B groups, with an unassigned category and a per-donor/group count table. The following method is frozen **before opening the full count matrix or seeing any correlation**:

1. Treat MatrixMarket row *i* as metadata row *i*. Require 4,861 unique barcodes, the expected four donor values, and exact agreement between each row's summed counts and its metadata `tscp_count` for at least 99% of rows. Stop if this order check fails. Keep all author-provided cells with positive library size; the author's pipeline has already filtered low-quality cells.
2. For assignment only, transform each count to `log1p(10000 * count / cell_library_total)`. Compute arithmetic mean marker scores: T lineage `{CD3D, CD3E, TRAC, CD2}`, B `{MS4A1, CD79A, CD79B, BANK1}`, classical monocyte `{LYZ, FCN1, S100A8, S100A9, CTSS}`, and NK exclusion `{NKG7, GNLY, KLRD1, FCGR3A}`. Each marker name must resolve to exactly one Ensembl row in `all_genes.csv`. Select the highest lineage only if its score is at least 0.55 and exceeds the runner-up by at least 0.20. Require a detected T anchor among `CD3D/CD3E/TRAC`, B anchor among `MS4A1/CD79A`, or monocyte anchors `LYZ` and at least one of `FCN1/S100A8/S100A9`. Label NK and cells failing any rule as `other` or `ambiguous`, with a reason.
3. Within T-lineage cells, score CD4 `{IL7R, CCR7, LTB, MAL}` and CD8 `{CD8A, GZMK, CCL5, GZMA}`. Assign CD4 or CD8 only when its score is at least 0.45, exceeds the other by at least 0.15, and has a detected anchor (`IL7R/CCR7/MAL` for CD4; `CD8A/GZMK` for CD8). Otherwise label `ambiguous_T`. None of the four held-out QC genes—`CD14`, `CD19`, `CD4`, `CD8B`—participates in assignment.
4. Require at least 25 cells for **each** donor × target-group pair and at most 65% `other`/ambiguous cells overall. In donor-group pseudobulks, require held-out marker CPM enrichment of at least 1.5-fold in at least three of four donors: `CD14` in classical monocytes versus all other target groups; `CD19` in B cells versus all other target groups; `CD4` in CD4 versus CD8 T cells; `CD8B` in CD8 versus CD4 T cells. Report per-donor group sizes, ambiguity reasons, assignment scores, and held-out enrichment as `label_qc.tsv`. These tests establish a useful marker-defined partition, not equivalence to FACS.
5. Use the unrestricted [Ensembl GRCh38 release 111 GTF](https://ftp.ensembl.org/pub/release-111/gtf/homo_sapiens/Homo_sapiens.GRCh38.111.gtf.gz) (54,350,257 B by HTTP `Content-Length`; [Ensembl data-use statement](https://sep2025.archive.ensembl.org/info/about/legal/disclaimer.html)). Strip only the Ensembl version suffix for stable-ID joins. Keep canonical chromosomes 1–22, X, Y and `gene_biotype=protein_coding`; define gene span in base pairs as inclusive `end - start + 1`. Require at least 15,000 unique protein-coding IDs in the Parse table after the join.
6. Sum raw UMI counts for each donor × group. Convert each resulting sample to counts per million using its own total across all genes; calculate `log2(CPM + 1)`. Use one common protein-coding gene universe: genes with CPM at least 1 in at least two donors in any one target group. Require at least 5,000 eligible nonconstant genes. For each gene and group, average the four donor `log2(CPM + 1)` values. Compute Pearson `r` between `log10(gene_span_bp)` and mean expression over the *same* eligible gene universe for every group, then rank groups by increasing `abs(r)`. The CD14-group `r` and weakest-absolute group are the two benchmark-derived decisions. Record `n_genes`, Pearson `p`, and donor count; do not tune filtering or transforms to change the ranking.

Produce cell assignments, donor pseudobulks, gene-length table, group means, all four correlations, and ranking by `abs(r)`. Grade intermediate identity/count/normalization/length artifacts as well as the two decisions. Do not claim that a marker-derived group is experimentally sorted or that the analysis reproduces the original benchmark's numerical answers.

**Gate in bounded remote source preparation:** test the frozen order/classification/overlap criteria above; pin upstream bytes by SHA256. If any gate fails, do not compute/report correlations or register a task; screen another explicitly licensed owner-published matrix. At method freeze time, no matrix had been downloaded, copied to GCS, or prepared.

## Other sources screened

- [GSE327707](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE327707) has a particularly close four-group, six-donor sorted bulk design, and the linked article declares processed data deposited there. The authors' MIT code repository does not contain the count matrix, and the GEO data object has no explicit license. It remains deferred.
- [Tabula Muris FACS counts](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Single-cell_RNA-seq_data_from_Smart-seq2_sequencing_of_FACS_sorted_cells_v2_/5829687) is directly CC BY 4.0 with author cell ontology labels and seven mice. It is a mouse tissue atlas, so it is a looser match to the four human PBMC endpoints.
- [Human Cell Landscape](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/HCL_DGE_Data/7235471) is directly CC BY 4.0 and has author cell labels, but its relevant count and annotation files are much larger and mix organs/stages; no compact PBMC-only subset was established.
- [ENCODE released bulk RNA-seq](https://www.encodeproject.org/about/data-use-policy/) has unrestricted-use terms and direct primary-cell labels. Released GRCh38/GENCODE V29 RSEM gene-quantification files were found for CD19-positive B cells ([ENCSR896YYL](https://www.encodeproject.org/experiments/ENCSR896YYL/), `ENCFF192WQE` and `ENCFF740YHF`, two isogenic replicates from donor `ENCDO383AQS`), CD4 T cells ([ENCSR411MUF](https://www.encodeproject.org/experiments/ENCSR411MUF/), `ENCFF954SRP`, donor `ENCDO394DKX`), CD8 T cells ([ENCSR151FXS](https://www.encodeproject.org/experiments/ENCSR151FXS/) and [ENCSR501DTN](https://www.encodeproject.org/experiments/ENCSR501DTN/), `ENCFF494PJN` and `ENCFF677IBW`, both donor `ENCDO781BMZ`), and CD14-positive monocytes ([ENCSR905LVO](https://www.encodeproject.org/experiments/ENCSR905LVO/), `ENCFF408GAO`, donor `ENCDO028NGF`). Each TSV is about 11 MB, and the same bulk RSEM pipeline generated these V29 quantifications. The B and CD8 repeats are isogenic, the CD4 and monocyte groups have one sample each, and the cell types come from different donors and studies. Other monocyte assays either use a different RNA selection or the newer Altius quantification pipeline. These files are individually usable but do not support a balanced, matched four-group comparison. No ENCODE expression-derived effective length should be substituted for genomic span from a matched GTF. No ENCODE files were downloaded, and no task or endpoint credit is claimed from this screen.
