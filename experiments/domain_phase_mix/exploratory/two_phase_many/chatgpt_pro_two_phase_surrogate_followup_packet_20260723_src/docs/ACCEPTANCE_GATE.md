# Frozen Acceptance Gate

Freeze this gate before generating follow-up candidates.

A headline candidate must satisfy all applicable conditions:

1. Introduce a materially new phase-specific state, transition, invariant, or identification argument rather than reparameterizing a rejected route.
2. Improve at least one primary target-matched heldout decision or calibration diagnostic beyond paired-bootstrap uncertainty.
3. Improve both targets, or improve at least two primary diagnostics on one target without a material regression on the other.
4. Keep every core grouped-OOF RMSE within 5% of the relevant Pareto baseline.
5. Avoid worsening policy-matched heldout Regret@1 by more than 0.002 BPB.
6. Reduce or preserve optimism errors above 0.05 BPB on both targets.
7. Move observed-on-predicted calibration toward one without an unconstrained output calibrator.
8. Improve or preserve major proposal-stratum calibration, not only pooled calibration.
9. Survive exact-fiber, algebraically tied, independently refitted one-phase, and full two-phase comparisons.
10. Demonstrate stable parameter signs and comparable dimensionless values across folds or related panels.
11. Produce a plausible, bootstrap-stable raw asymmetric optimum before deployment regularization.
12. Survive nested ablation showing that each retained mechanism contributes on at least two independent panels or designs.

A rigorous non-identification or partial-identification theorem passes as a scientific result if it is supported by explicit finite-sample null-space or uncertainty evidence and changes the next experimental design. It does not count as a solved point optimum.

All recommendations remain provisional until tested on a newly sealed confirmation panel.
