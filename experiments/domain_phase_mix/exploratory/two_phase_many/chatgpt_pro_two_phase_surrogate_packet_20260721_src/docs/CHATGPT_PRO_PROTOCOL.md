# ChatGPT Pro Protocol

## Objective

Use the packet to discover or rigorously rule out a concise mechanistic surrogate that improves two-phase mixture selection. The task is about surface correctness and decision quality, not only interpolation.

## Snapshot

At build time the packet contains 280-row one- and two-phase fit swarms at both 300M and Delphi 3e18, 1,533 complete 3e18 heldout observations over 1,487 unique coordinates, two dense StarCoder surfaces, an 840-row production swarm, a legacy 60M panel, exact historical model sources, and the prior approach registries. Every canonical 3e18 heldout observation has both primary targets.

The 3e18 archive contains designed interventions, repeated coordinates, candidate sweeps, one-phase policies, fixed-aggregate phase-order panels, adversarially proposed policies, and historical deployment candidates. It is not exchangeable. Preserve `training_series`, `proposal_target`, `candidate_kind`, `policy_class`, `group_id`, and support-distance strata in evaluation.

## Development Loop

1. Verify packet integrity and reproduce a baseline.
2. Freeze a candidate batch using algebra, StarCoder surfaces, grouped CV, fit-complement checks, and cross-swarm evidence.
3. Append the freeze and candidate definitions to `DATA_USE_LEDGER.csv` before reading heldout outcomes for that batch.
4. Evaluate the whole frozen batch once on exposed development heldouts.
5. Reject routes that fail their preregistered signature. Do not patch coefficients against individual heldout rows.
6. Promote at most three families to full optimization and adversarial review.
7. Design, but do not launch, a new untouched confirmation panel.

## Required Distinctions

- Full two-phase fit versus algebraically tied restriction of that fit.
- Algebraically tied restriction versus independent fit of the restricted form on one-phase rows.
- Target-matched heldout evaluation versus cross-target transfer.
- Fit-panel OOF metrics versus coordinate-disjoint development-heldout metrics.
- Raw-surrogate optimum versus deployment-regularized optimum.
- Empirical-support distance versus convex-support distance.
- Prediction calibration versus uncertainty or abstention diagnostics.

## Existing Compact Falsifier

Compact retained state is useful because it is simple and its proposed policy path becomes stable, but its raw surface remains optimistic. The 280-row Uncheatable optimum has predicted/observed BPB 0.953118/0.990255. The 280-row Table-9 optimum has predicted/observed BPB 0.951460/1.071885. Additional fit rows do not remove the bias. A successor must explain why these policies are overcredited rather than merely rescale their outputs after seeing the result.

## Sealed Evidence

A 140-policy Compact sub-280 optimum-learning-curve panel is running outside the packet. Its outcomes are deliberately excluded. Do not attempt to recover them. The panel was designed before this ChatGPT Pro effort and must remain untouched evidence for later comparison.

## Output Discipline

The returned solution must contain executable code and explicit data-use provenance. A negative result is acceptable and should still include a tested zip. Do not claim confirmation from any exposed row in this packet.
