## Corrections and qualifications to the review

1. **Corpus versions.** CC inferred a source-version mismatch from the `dolma3_` archive labels. That is unsupported. `two_phase_dolma3_dolmino_top_level.py:200-201` reuses `finemath_3_plus-a26b0f` and `dolma/wiki-212315`; `pretraining_datasets/dolma3_pool.py:9-21` explicitly says its Wikipedia is Dolma v1.7 and its FineMath is the HF 3+ source. What changes here is tokenization, sampled support, geometry and mixture weights. Do not claim a corpus-version change from the wrapper names.
2. **Causal interpretation.** CC's suggested matched-minus-unmatched contrast at fixed p would be a finite-support contrast. Calling it a pure repetition penalty would ignore changed available diversity and conditional subset composition. That contrast is excluded from the current scope. The requested target/matched comparison measures conditional optima and selection transfer.
3. **Noise.** Recalculation confirms CC's PALOMA pooled SDs 0.00953865 and 0.00640037. However, the paired p70-minus-p50 differences have mean 0.00836756 and SD 0.00365544, with all six positive (`review_checks.json`). The crossed trainer/subset structure and common-randomness pairing matter. Those PALOMA statistics do not establish that future Uncheatable refinement is guaranteed to fail. No universal noise bands or significance thresholds for new domains are imported from StarCoder.
4. **Objective coverage.** Removing different component sets for different domains changes the comparison objective. The specification instead fixes the same secondary component set for every curve. This is a sensitivity analysis, not a causal proof or a way to select a favorable objective.
5. **Transfer and tokenizer assertions.** One observed proxy-to-target optimum shift is not a mathematical lower bound on target optima. The quoted 1.15–1.3 tokenizer expansion factor is also not measured in this package. Neither assertion is adopted.
6. **Range and boundary predictions.** The historical optima occurred at much lower weights: Wikipedia around 0.0021 and FineMath around 0.0389. That weakens their predictive value here. It does not establish that all three new curves will have left-boundary minima. Boundary and flat results remain reportable.
7. **Extra subset.** A four-times-smaller subset would no longer match the frozen target's repetition. It is excluded, along with additional subset draws and unmatched controls, under the user's one-sweep scope.
8. **Numerical evaluation.** The final review says the PALOMA restoration tolerance bounds reduction differences and that these cancel in target regret. That is too strong: the PALOMA check does not bound Uncheatable error, and model-dependent reduction errors need not cancel in a loss difference. Record evaluation batch sizes and keep the scoring population, aggregation and within-scale policy fixed; do not claim exact cancellation.

The final review requested one wording correction: name the experiment a mixture sweep and define the question as preferred mixture fraction along a fixed exposure path, with epochs reported at the chosen fraction. This is now applied. The figure caption must disclose StarCoder's different measured grid. No additional experimental arms or repetitions were requested by the closing review or added after it.

The survey can locate observed conditional minima on its sampled grid; it cannot guarantee a precise continuous optimum or demonstrate that every static upper cap fails. The target geometry and finite parent memberships remain fixed. The initial target runs use the direct parent with natural repetition; the matched proxies use its nested subset. Additional experiments are not prerequisites for obtaining these exploratory results.
