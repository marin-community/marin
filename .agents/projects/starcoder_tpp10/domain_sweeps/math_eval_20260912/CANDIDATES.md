# Candidate domains for separated proxy optima

FineMath's completed math evaluation already supplies an observed minimum above ten epochs: both MATH-500 and GSM8K reference-solution perplexity minimize at 11.10 epochs, with worse scores at 7.94 and 15.83. The current complete Wikipedia and FineMath proxy curves both minimize common Uncheatable at 4.77 epochs. Wikipedia's own Wikipedia-English evaluation minimizes at 7.94. These facts establish evaluation-dependent preference within FineMath; they do not yet establish cross-domain separation under a common math evaluation. No additional proxy or target training is released.

## What the archive supports

In the historical 60M focal-dose panel, Dolmino synthetic math, code and thinking have their lowest observed OlmoBaseEval loss at 14.486 epochs. Their Uncheatable minima are 7.243, 7.243 and 1.811 epochs respectively. None of the 39 focal domains has an observed Uncheatable minimum above 10 epochs. The historical panel used a different model, mixture background, pools, exposure regime and evaluation; its numerical optima are not predictions for these 16.6M proxies. In particular, the historical synthetic math mixture is not Nemotron Math Textbooks. The full minimum table is archived in archived_domain_minima.json with its source path in the original domain-extension evidence.

The three 14.486-epoch minima are interior observed minima: the archive also measures 28.971 epochs, where all three are worse. For synthetic math the broader-suite losses are 1.24119, 1.23134 and 1.23993 at 7.243, 14.486 and 28.971 epochs. These values correct the first CC review's mistaken description of 14.486 as the last grid point. A 57,336-byte footer read also verifies a plain `text` column in the actual regional Nemotron Math Textbooks payload; its schema is recorded in nemotron_schema.json.

## Decision and figure

The next proposed step is to evaluate existing Wikipedia proxies on the same MATH-500/GSM8K protocol, before commissioning another math domain. This would measure whether a common math objective separates the two domains without new training. A zero-Wikipedia minimum would indicate that adding Wikipedia is unhelpful for that objective, rather than an interior repetition turnover. That extra evaluation is a proposal, not part of the completed release.

For any agreed candidate, retain N=16,587,008, D=165,937,152, TPP10, the same six-web background, 10,485,760 focus tokens and one seed/subset. Retain a fixed 190,316,544-token parent definition for any later target expansion. Sweep proxies only. A seven-point nonzero grid costs 1.74362e17 training FLOPs per domain, using the existing shared web-only baseline; preparation, evaluation and compilation are additional. The 70% point is about 11.1 epochs, and 100% about 15.8. A best score at 100% is a boundary minimum, so extending the range would need a separately agreed design.

Choose an illustrative pair using complete curves and a stated evaluation, and retain every screened domain and metric in the appendix. Show the observed points and minima; use loss above each curve's own minimum if the level offsets obscure the shape. State that the figure illustrates conditional optimum differences under fixed model, budget, pool and background. Changing the focus weight changes exposure and replacement of web data together; it does not measure an intrinsic domain repetition constant or prove that every shared cap fails. If different evaluations are used for the curves, label that explicitly and do not present the separation as a shared-objective result.

Before the math results, CC favored Nemotron Math Textbooks plus Stack-Edu Python, with arXiv as backup. After reviewing the complete results, CC agrees that scoring existing Wikipedia proxies on the same math evaluation comes first and deprioritizes a second math-domain sweep. If additional separation remains useful, my order is Stack-Edu Python, then arXiv, with Nemotron Textbooks retained for a deliberately different math-content hypothesis. No candidate is guaranteed to prefer >10 epochs on Uncheatable. No training has been submitted for any shortlisted candidate.
