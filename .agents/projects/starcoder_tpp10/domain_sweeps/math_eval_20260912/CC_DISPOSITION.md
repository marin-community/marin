CC's follow-up checked the repair. The retry evaluates exactly the same eight checkpoints and scored token populations.

Two candidate claims required correction. The archived synthetic math/code/thinking curves include worse 28.971-epoch points after their 14.486-epoch minima, so those minima are interior observed points. The regional Nemotron Math Textbooks Parquet schema contains plain text, verified with a 57,336-byte footer read. CC accepted both corrections and withdrew its unsupported prediction that FineMath-4+ would minimize near p30.

Both reviews agree that no archived domain establishes >10 optimal epochs on common Uncheatable. The corrected shortlist for discussion is Nemotron Math Textbooks and Stack-Edu Python, with Proof-Pile2 arXiv as backup. Dataset descriptions motivate hypotheses; they do not predict the ordering of optima in the current tiny-model setting. Existing Wikipedia and FineMath curves remain part of the screening record.
