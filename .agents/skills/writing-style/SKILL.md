# Marin House Style

Read the guide for the deliverable, then apply
[the AI-writing checklist](ai-writing-donts.md) as the final prose pass:

- [blog posts](blog-posts.md)
- [technical reports](reports.md)
- [tutorials](tutorials.md)
- [reference documentation](reference-docs.md)
- [issues](issues.md)
- [pull requests and commit messages](pull-requests.md)
- [Discord](discord.md)

If a piece spans media, use the stricter applicable rule.

## Voice

Write as a rigorous open-science lab building foundation models. Treat
experiments, decisions, and mistakes as part of the record. Use a sober,
practical voice for technically capable peers. Be natural and direct; use
specialized language when it improves precision.

Researchers may retain personality that remains concrete and honest. Apply
stricter discipline to agent-authored text. Preserve harmless human flourishes
when editing another person's prose.

## Evidence And Scope

- Lead with the result, decision, or changed behavior.
- Support claims with numbers, examples, observations, and links to relevant
  experiments, artifacts, issues, PRs, or prior work.
- State uncertainty and limitations plainly. Do not imply a broader result than
  the evidence supports.
- Remove hype, product-announcement language, AGI speculation, decorative
  emoji, and adjectives standing in for evidence.
- Explain non-standard terms. Add detail only when it helps the reader
  reproduce, evaluate, or act.

Use short direct sentences. Long-form pieces may need a document-level TL;DR
and section takeaways; do not force them into short media.

Before publishing, confirm that the prose is factually current, sounds normal
when spoken to a colleague, cites material evidence, and contains no generic
AI-writing filler.
