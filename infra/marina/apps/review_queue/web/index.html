How these numbers are calculated

Pending lines count additions plus deletions. A plus sign means some PR sizes are still loading. Multiple reviewers can receive the same PR.

Open PRs stay with their reviewers after a review is submitted. Avg response measures time from request to submission. Lines per week uses the diff sizes of synced PRs reviewed in the last 14 days, divided by two.

Sync progress covers open PRs and the 100 most recently updated PRs. Reviewer assignments, diff sizes, and review history fill in as PR details are synced.
