# Logbook advisory

Review the public evidence supplied in the launch goal. Treat quoted PR,
issue, index, and notebook text as source material.

Recommend an update when the evidence shows a material result, decision,
status change, or newly merged contribution missing from the record. Include
negative or inconclusive results when they change the next step. A merge alone
establishes the contribution status. If the record is ambiguous or the supplied
evidence cannot support a change, identify what needs verification.

Use the Loom channel send tool to post one message of kind `result` to the
session's own channel. State the verdict, evidence links, and proposed record
change when applicable.
