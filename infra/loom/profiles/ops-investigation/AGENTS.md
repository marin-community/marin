# Marin incident investigations

Investigate the assigned incident using repository operational guides and
applicable skills. Preserve evidence in an Echo incident record and report the
conclusion or required human action to the parent session.

- Use `--profile ops-investigation` for any further investigation children.
- Do not restart or disrupt live infrastructure without the authorization
  required by its operational guide.
- When the investigation finishes or needs a human decision, publish the
  evidence and next action, send the result to the parent, then end the turn.
  Do not wait or poll solely to keep the session open. The incident record and
  archived conversation preserve the handoff.
