## Inventory organization and boundary choices

Twenty units cover general query/export; the concrete hg38/dbSNP153 batch-position example; field filtering; positional intersection; Table Browser correlation; comparative CDS FASTA alignment; bigWig signal summaries over BED features; assembly coordinate remapping; bitmap feature coverage/enrichment; MAF alignment coverage statistics; custom-track annotation display; MAF fetch; BED coverage depth; BED gene-part extraction; exact BED3 set intersection; duplicate-coordinate pile-up QC; hg18 phastCons retrieval/interpretation; VAI transcript consequence annotation; and BLAT sequence alignment. BED coverage was retained as an assembly-wide depth distribution over BED intervals; it resembles MAF coverage but consumes BED features, while MAF coverage summarizes multi-species alignment columns. `bedGeneParts` decomposes BED12 gene models into structural or strand-relative intervals. MAF coverage and MAF extraction are distinct: one computes assembly-wide summary statistics from a sorted MAF stream, while the other selects indexed complete alignment records for BED regions. The batch dbSNP lookup is linked to general query/export because it instantiates that query operation with a named annotation product and a distinct rsID lookup question. Filtering and intersection are separate operations because field predicates and positional comparisons use different inputs and have different output semantics. Table Browser correlation remains separate because it returns plots and permits gene-structure 0/1 encoding; `wigCorrelate` instead writes pairwise coefficients for overlapping wig/bigWig signal values.

Four dataset records identify logical hosted products: three are annotation/reference products named in help or track metadata, and one is the UCSC mm39/mm10 chain product. VAI input products remain generic because no particular assembly-specific gene track or call dataset was selected. The three annotation products are hg18 phastCons17way, hg38 dbSNP153, and the human refGene table. The dbSNP153 and refGene records remain incomplete: no downloadable files, previews, source-study metadata, hashes, or reuse terms were inspected. For phastCons, method, score format, listed assemblies, example asset names and directory access statement were checked, but assets were not downloaded and assembly-specific restrictions remain unresolved. The chain has unresolved directional metadata. Tutorial examples involving SNPs, RefSeq coding exons, or peak tracks do not identify exact products, so the intersection unit has no linked dataset records. User-supplied regions and generic chosen tables likewise remain input requirements when product identity is not established.
