## Relationships and useful combinations

`intersect` can provide overlap annotations or subsets consumed by `coverage`, `jaccard`, or `fisher`; `complement` and `multiinter -empty` both expose uncovered genomic segments but have different inputs and partitions. `coverage` summarizes breadth/depth on defined A intervals, whereas `genomecov` reports genome-wide depth distribution and can be chained with `intersect` to restrict zero-depth spans to capture targets. `closest` answers nearest-feature questions and `reldist` summarizes a distribution against two nearest intervals, so they are related but not duplicates. `map` aggregates B value columns over A overlaps; `merge` collapses spans; `shuffle` generates constrained null placements. `getfasta` connects interval coordinates to a reference sequence but no identified reference asset was examined. The tutorial's Jaccard pairwise matrix is a larger composition involving external sample files, GNU parallel, a script, and R; resolve these sources before treating it as an author-ready task.
