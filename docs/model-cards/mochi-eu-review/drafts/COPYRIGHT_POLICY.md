## Scope and responsibility

This policy covers copyright and related rights in data collection, acquisition, preprocessing and training for Marin Mochi, and the handling of complaints concerning those activities or model outputs. The responsible owner maintains the policy and coordinates data, release and legal decisions.

The model's OpenMDW 1.1 license grants permissions concerning the released model. It does not establish permission to reproduce third-party works or authorize infringing use of model outputs.

## Data acquisition and rights reservations

Before admitting a source to a training build, the data owner records its provider, version, acquisition date, selected content, access conditions and applicable rights information. Public availability alone is insufficient evidence of training rights. Unresolved restrictions are referred to the policy owner before use.

For collection performed by the provider or on its behalf, the data owner documents lawful access and identifies and respects applicable reservations under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 using appropriate, current technical measures. The approved collection procedure must specify crawler identity, robots.txt behavior and other applicable rights-reservation signals, access restrictions and exclusions. Collection must not bypass effective paywalls or other technological access protections.

For third-party datasets, the data owner reviews the provider's terms, provenance and available information about collection and rights-reservation handling. A dataset license is assessed together with the underlying content and applicable law. Records must distinguish direct collection from third-party redistribution and preserve any conditions requiring action by Marin.

For historical Mochi training, the provider confirms that it relied on upstream dataset providers' collection and rights-reservation handling and did not operate or commission crawling. Consultation with Common Crawl about its independently created Focus Crawl did not make that crawl provider-operated collection. This historical description does not assert independent verification of every upstream practice.

## Use of the released model

The accompanying model documentation alerts users that copyright-infringing uses are prohibited by applicable law. Users must assess rights before reproducing, distributing or otherwise using generated material.

The policy owner assesses the risk of infringing reproduction and selects proportionate technical and operational safeguards for the model and any provider-operated services. Known limitations and untested safeguards are documented. The current exports have not undergone full inference parity or post-export generation validation; this policy makes no claim of output filtering or reliable suppression of protected training content.

## Rightsholder complaints

Rightsholders and their authorised representatives may submit a complaint through the contact above. A useful complaint identifies the work or right, the complainant's authority, the relevant source/model version or output, the alleged issue and sufficient supporting information. Do not send unlawful content or unnecessary personal data through the complaint channel.

The responsible owner records the complaint, requests clarification where needed, investigates relevant acquisition and release records, and responds diligently and within a reasonable time. Review must be consistent and non-arbitrary. Decisions and corrective actions are recorded, including source exclusions, changes to future training inputs, documentation corrections or other measures appropriate to the case. No particular remedy, retraining or removal from already distributed weights is promised in advance.

## Maintenance and public information

The policy owner reviews this policy when relevant law, collection practices, training sources or material complaints change. The release owner keeps the training-content summary aligned with the covered model versions and provides stable links from their cards. Policy changes do not retrospectively establish that an earlier training build used the new measures.
