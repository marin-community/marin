# Research Notes

Use this brief to frame a short technical Research Note. The [blog guide](blog-posts.md) covers outlines, structure, and review.

Share a result, observation, or engineering lesson that may be useful to other
researchers.

Lead with the result. Explain what was tested, why it was tested if relevant,
what happened, and what the evidence supports. Include concrete experimental
details, comparisons, figures, and links wherever available.

Use direct, natural, technically precise prose. Assume an audience of ML
researchers and engineers. Explain unfamiliar concepts when needed, but don't
belabor the basics.

Keep the length proportional to the substance. A few paragraphs are fine. A
substantial result may warrant a longer explanation.

Be candid about limitations, negative results, and unresolved questions.
Distinguish observations from interpretations. Do not invent motivations,
causal explanations, experimental details, or broader implications.

Avoid promotional language, generic introductions, formulaic conclusions,
exaggerated significance, and characteristic LLM rhetorical constructions.

Prefer an informative, modest research note to an impressive-sounding blog
post.

**The objective is to make useful research knowledge public with minimal
additional work.** Keep the claims proportional to the evidence.
